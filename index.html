Research & Projects

FloodShield, Flood Resilience Sensor Project
2023 – Present

Co-developed a low-cost, waterproof Arduino-based flood early-warning sensor system with a target cost below $10 per unit. Integrated NASA TRMM and GPM rainfall data with ground-level measurements and tested sensor designs in flood-affected communities.

Satellite-Based Flood Prediction Research
2024

Analyzed NASA MODIS and Sentinel spectral data alongside rainfall records to investigate early indicators of flooding in South Asian river basins. Developed a rainfall-to-flood correlation approach and reported approximately 38% improvement in validation accuracy.

Morphing Wing UAV Research
Lumiere Education · Purdue Mentorship · 2025

Studied servo, linear, and ball-screw actuators for morphing UAV wings. Modeled actuator and NACA airfoil combinations and evaluated stroke capability, blocking force, power consumption, lifecycle cost, and aerodynamic trade-offs.
