01 / ABOUT

Building toward useful engineering.

My work sits between electronics, physical systems, and environmental problems. I’m especially interested in understanding how devices work at the hardware level and how those devices can become part of larger energy and sensing systems.

I’m currently studying Electrical Engineering at NJIT, with interests spanning semiconductor devices, energy systems, embedded hardware, and computer engineering.

Before college, I worked on projects involving flood sensing, satellite data, UAV design, and environmental monitoring. Those projects taught me to approach engineering as a combination of physical hardware, data, and experimentation.

03 / PROJECTS

Things I've built and investigated.

A selection of engineering projects and research work from before and during my transition into electrical engineering.

01

FloodShield

Arduino · Sensors · NASA Data

Developed a low-cost waterproof flood early-warning sensor system under $10, combining ground-level measurements with NASA TRMM and GPM rainfall data for flood-risk monitoring.

02

Satellite-Based Flood Prediction

MODIS · Sentinel · Remote Sensing

Investigated satellite spectral data and rainfall patterns to study relationships between environmental changes and local flooding, with a focus on improving early-warning approaches.

03

Morphing Wing UAV Research

CAD · NACA Airfoils · Aerodynamics

Compared servo, linear, and ball-screw actuators for morphing UAV wings across force, power, and cost metrics and modeled actuator-airfoil combinations.
